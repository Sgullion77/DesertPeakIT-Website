What you can learn

Everyday technology, explained clearly.

Choose a subject you want to feel more comfortable with. Lessons can focus on real tasks and questions rather than complicated technical terms.

Computer Basics

Practice using a computer, navigating programs, working with files, and finding your way around.

Email & Video Calls

Phones & Tablets

Explore common settings, apps, touchscreens, and everyday smartphone or tablet tasks.

Internet & Google Basics

Learn to browse websites, search for useful information, and find reliable online resources.

A patient approach

Frequently asked questions

New to technology? You're welcome here.

Do I need to know anything about computers before starting?

No. You can start with the basics and focus on the things you'd most like to learn.

Can a family member inquire for me?

Absolutely. A family member or caregiver can reach out with questions about the education program.

Can I ask about a specific phone, tablet, or computer?

Yes. Describe your device and the topics you're interested in so we can discuss the best next step.

Curiosity is a great place to start.
